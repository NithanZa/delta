Plans and pricing

Simple per-channel pricing for every stage of your business. Pay for the channels you connect and nothing else. All prices below are billed annually.

Essentials. $5 per channel per month. Unlimited scheduled posts. Unlimited ideas. One user account. AI Assistant. Advanced analytics. Engagement inbox. Hashtag manager. First comment scheduling. World-class customer support.

Team. $10 per channel per month. Everything in Essentials. Unlimited team members. Access levels and permissions. Content approval workflows. Draft collaboration tools. Shared saved replies.

Agency. $100 per month for 10 channels. Everything in Team. Custom access and permissions for every client. Agency-friendly onboarding. Priority support with a two-hour response target. Additional channels for $5 per channel per month.

All three plans are billed annually. Monthly billing is not available on this page; contact sales if your organization requires monthly invoicing.

Frequently asked questions
Why is everything billed annually? Annual billing keeps our prices lower and lets us invest in support. The prices shown are the effective monthly rate; the full year is charged at the start of the term.
Can I add channels mid-year? Yes. Added channels are prorated for the remainder of your term at the same per-channel rate.
Is there a free trial? Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You are not charged until the trial ends.
What if I need to cancel? You can cancel at any time and your plan stays active until the end of the paid term. Refunds are available within the first 30 days.
Do you offer a free plan? A free plan with three channels and ten scheduled posts per channel is available from the sign-up page.
