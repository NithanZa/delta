Volume pricing for larger workspaces. The per-channel price on Essentials and Team steps down as you connect more channels. Channels 1 to 9: full price. Channels 10 to 24: $4 per channel per month on Team. Channels 25 to 49: $3 per channel per month on Team. Channels 50 and above: $2 per channel per month on Team. Volume pricing is applied automatically on your invoice; there is nothing to configure. Example: a Team workspace with 30 channels billed yearly pays $10 for each of the first 9 channels, $4 for channels 10 to 24, and $3 for channels 25 to 30.

Frequently asked questions
What are the billing options for Buffer's paid plans? You can choose to pay monthly or yearly. Yearly billing saves you two months.
Do you have a plan for agencies? Agencies use the Team plan with a channel per client account, and volume pricing applies automatically once a workspace has 10 or more channels.
What happens if I remove a channel? Your next invoice reflects the lower channel count. You never pay for a channel you have disconnected.
Do you offer refunds? Yes, within 30 days of any payment.
