Start Page by Buffer. Your link in bio, beautifully simple.
Create a free Start Page in minutes. Start Page is a free, mobile-friendly landing page that lives at the link in your bio and brings together everything you want your audience to see: your latest posts, your shop, your newsletter, your booking link and more.

Everything you need in one link
Add links, images, videos, text blocks and embedded content with a few taps. Choose from a collection of templates and color themes, or match your brand with custom colors and fonts. Changes publish instantly, so your page is always up to date.

Built for creators, small businesses and the agencies that support them
Start Page is included with every Buffer plan, including the Free plan. Agencies and freelancers can create a Start Page for each client from the same Buffer account and keep every page organized alongside the client's channels. Each page has its own analytics: views, clicks and the links your audience taps the most.

Connect it to your schedule
Publish a post in Buffer and add it to your Start Page at the same time, so your newest content is always one tap away for people arriving from your bio. Start Page works with Instagram, TikTok, Threads, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube and Facebook.

Pricing
Start Page is free. There is no limit on the number of visitors and no Buffer branding on paid plans. Custom domains are available on the Team plan.

Frequently asked questions
Is Start Page really free? Yes. Start Page is included with the Free plan and every paid plan.
Can I have more than one Start Page? Each channel group can have its own page, which is how agencies manage a page per client.
Does Start Page work on mobile? Pages are mobile-first and load in under a second on a typical connection.
Can I use my own domain? Custom domains are available on the Team plan; Free and Essentials pages live at a start.page address.
How do I get started? Log in to Buffer, open Start Page from the sidebar and choose a template. Your page is live as soon as you publish it.
