Buffer Affiliate Program. Earn by recommending the tool you already use.

Join thousands of creators, marketers and agencies who earn a recurring commission every time someone they refer becomes a Buffer customer.

How it works
1. Apply in two minutes. Tell us where you plan to share Buffer: your blog, newsletter, YouTube channel, podcast, course or client work.
2. Get your link. Once approved, you receive a unique referral link and a dashboard that tracks clicks, trials and conversions in real time.
3. Earn 20% recurring commission. For every customer who signs up for a paid Buffer plan through your link, you earn 20% of their subscription payments for the first 12 months. Commissions are paid monthly by PayPal or bank transfer once your balance reaches $50.

Who the program is for
Content creators who review social media tools. Marketing consultants and agencies who set up Buffer for clients. Course instructors and community leaders teaching social media. Software companies with complementary products.

What you get
A 90-day cookie window, so you are credited for sign-ups up to three months after someone clicks your link. Ready-made banners, screenshots and copy in the affiliate dashboard. A dedicated partner manager for affiliates who refer more than ten customers a month. Early access to product announcements so your content stays accurate.

Program rules
Self-referrals are not eligible. Paid search advertising on Buffer brand terms is not permitted. Coupons and discount codes may not be offered unless provided by Buffer. Referrals must be real people who intend to use Buffer. We review accounts periodically and reserve the right to withhold commissions that violate these rules.

Agencies: a program built for you
If you manage client accounts in Buffer, the Agency Partner Program may be a better fit. It offers a higher 25% recurring commission on clients you bring to Buffer, a private community of agency partners, early access to features and priority support. You can be a member of both programs, but a single referral is credited once.

Frequently asked questions
How long do commissions last? 20% of each referred customer's payments for 12 months from their first payment.
Is there a minimum payout? Yes, $50. Balances below the minimum roll over to the next month.
Can I promote Buffer in multiple languages? Yes. Our checkout is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German.
How do I apply? Click Join the program, fill in the form, and we will review your application within five business days.
