Delta — how I'm approaching the build

StepFun × NYU PMC Case Jam · Track 2 · deadline Sun Nov 1, 4:00 PM ET · written Fri Oct 31, night

One sentence

Delta is an AI competitive-intelligence analyst that turns dated public pages into facts with verbatim quotes, connects them into hypotheses that get strengthened, contradicted and revised across runs, and produces company-specific insights and actions — with StepFun doing all the analytical work.

The demo story (locked)

Your company: “Loop”

A small social-media scheduling tool that sells to marketing agencies and freelancers. Priority: win agencies. Constraint: tiny team, cannot match a big roadmap.

Competitor: Buffer (real)

Public history has an actual reversal on the agency segment — the exact example in the brief, with real, dated, unedited pages.

Run 1 · pages ≤ Dec 2023

Agency plan exists ($120/mo, 10 channels), agency landing page, feature-comparison support article. Hypothesis: “Buffer is investing in agencies as a segment and channel.”

Run 2 · pages ≤ Dec 2024

Agency plan cut to $100, support docs recommend it for 11+ channels, CEO note on per-channel "New Buffer". Hypothesis strengthened; the price cut is detected by diffing two captures of the same URL.

Run 3 · pages ≤ Jan 2026

Agency plan gone from pricing (Oct 2025), “last updated November 2025”, yet an Agency Partner Program, a “built for agencies” post and two agency case studies in 2026. Hypothesis contradicted → revised: agencies are served via per-channel Team pricing and a partner channel, not a dedicated tier.

Insight for Loop

What happened / what it might mean / why it matters to Loop / what to do next — e.g. investigate whether agencies now feel underserved, with interview questions, before any product investment.

How StepFun is used (the 25% rubric line)

Snapshot (seeded or live URL)→ step-3.7-flash extract facts + verbatim quotes; diff same-URL snapshots→ step-5-preview (1M ctx) update hypotheses, timeline, strategy profile, open questions→ step-5-preview company-specific insights + respond/investigate actions
  • Every model call is JSON-mode, validated with Zod, retried once with the validation error. Fabricated quotes are dropped by a verbatim check.
  • The model must cite fact ids for every hypothesis update and insight; user corrections are passed in as authoritative.
  • Nothing downstream can tell a seeded page from a live-fetched one. UI says “Replaying snapshots captured on …”.

Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, cacheComponents off) · TypeScript · Tailwind v4 · SQLite via Drizzle (one file, zips for submission) · openai SDK pointed at api.stepfun.ai · Readability + jsdom for extraction · Vitest. Local-first, no auth, no deploy.
