group.one acquires SocialPilot to expand its digital marketing suite for small businesses and agencies
Press release, July 10, 2025

Copenhagen and Ahmedabad: group.one, the European web hosting and digital services group, today announced that it has acquired SocialPilot, the social media management platform used by more than 13,500 agencies and small businesses. The transaction closed today. Financial terms were not disclosed.

SocialPilot will continue to operate under its own brand and leadership. Founder and CEO Jimit Bagadiya will lead the business as part of group.one's marketing software division, and the SocialPilot team in Ahmedabad and remote locations joins group.one in full.

"SocialPilot has built one of the most capable and fairly priced social media management tools for agencies," said Fredrik Nilsson, CEO of group.one. "Bringing it into group.one lets us offer the small businesses we already serve with websites, email and hosting a complete way to publish, engage and report on social media, and it gives SocialPilot the distribution of a group that serves more than two million customers across Europe."

"Joining group.one is the right next chapter for SocialPilot," said Jimit Bagadiya. "Our customers will see the same product, the same team and the same pricing. What changes is the pace at which we can build: more integrations, more AI features, and a route to the millions of small businesses that group.one works with every day."

About the acquisition
SocialPilot's existing plans, pricing and customer contracts are unchanged. Customers do not need to take any action. The platform's roadmap for 2025 and 2026, including AI Pilot, the renamed AI Assistant, and deeper white-label reporting, continues as planned, and group.one expects to invest in the engineering team over the coming year.

About SocialPilot
Founded in 2014, SocialPilot helps marketing agencies and small businesses schedule content, collaborate with clients and report results across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Google Business Profile and Threads. SocialPilot is known for flat-rate pricing that includes client management and white-label reports on agency plans.

About group.one
group.one is a leading European provider of web hosting, domains, email and digital marketing tools for small businesses, with brands including one.com, Zone and Hostnet. Headquartered in Copenhagen, group.one serves more than two million customers and is majority owned by Cinven.
