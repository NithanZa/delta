Frequently asked questions
What are the billing options for Buffer's paid plans? You can choose to pay for your plan on a monthly or annual basis. By selecting annual billing, you'll receive the equivalent of two months free compared to paying monthly. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your billing settings; if you switch from monthly to annual, the remaining balance of your current month is credited toward the annual charge.
Do you offer refunds? We offer a full refund within the first 30 days of any paid plan, no questions asked.
What counts as a channel? Each social account you connect counts as one channel. Example: 2 Instagram accounts, 2 Facebook Pages and 2 LinkedIn Pages count as 6 channels.
Can I try Buffer before paying? Every paid plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start.
Is there a plan for agencies? Agencies use the Team plan and add a channel per client account. Unlimited team members and client collaborators are included, so you only pay for channels.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits? Yes, registered nonprofits receive 50% off any paid plan. Contact support with proof of status.
Which payment methods do you accept? We accept all major credit cards and, for annual plans, invoiced bank transfer on request.
